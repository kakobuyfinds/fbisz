The quick answer

A PikoBuy spreadsheet helps organize product discovery. Its value comes from clear source links, identifiable variants and useful comparison notes. Treat each row as a starting point: reopen the listing, check the measurements and confirm the full order conditions before committing.

FIELD NOTE 01 / FIT & PROPORTION

PikoBuy sizing guide: compare the garment, not the letter

A familiar size label can conceal an unfamiliar cut. Put a garment you already like on a flat surface, measure it without stretching and write down the measuring points. Then compare those numbers with the seller's chart for the exact item.

For a T-shirt, shoulder width, chest width and length describe more than “oversized.” For hoodies and sweaters, add sleeve length and consider the layer underneath. A jacket needs enough room for that layer without making the shoulders uncomfortably wide.

  • Pants and shorts: check the waist method, rise, inseam and leg opening. An elastic waistband may have both relaxed and stretched measurements.
  • Matching sets and jerseys: compare each piece separately. A suitable top does not guarantee suitable bottoms, and jersey cuts vary.
  • Underwear: resolve sizing and hygiene-related return questions before ordering. Read the current seller and service terms.
FIELD NOTE 02 / PHOTOS & EVIDENCE

PikoBuy QC photos: turn a concern into a specific request

Begin with identity: does the photographed item match the saved product ID, colour and size? Then ask what the image can actually answer. A top-down view may help compare shape; a close-up can show stitching; a ruler placed on the relevant dimension can clarify a measurement.

For colour comparison, ask for consistent lighting and avoid judging a subtle shade from one compressed photograph. For garment inspection, look at seams, cuffs and print placement. For accessories, request both the closure and the contact surfaces.

Save the source listing and selected options beside the photos so you are comparing the same variant. Ask whether extra images are available and whether they incur a charge. Visible condition checks do not authenticate a brand or prove how the item will wear.

FIELD NOTE 03 / COST & CONTEXT

PikoBuy shipping estimate: give every cost its own line

A product price and a delivered total answer different questions. Make separate entries for the item, domestic transport, service charges, international freight and any destination charges. Record the quote's currency and what is included before comparing options.

Packed weight and parcel dimensions can affect freight. Some routes use dimensional weight, calculated from package volume with a carrier-specific divisor. Ask which weight basis applies; do not borrow a divisor from an unrelated shipping example.

If comparing delivery to the US, UK or an EU country, use the exact destination and current route conditions. Check handling stages, tracking, coverage exclusions and return arrangements. Recalculate after a size, item quantity or packaging change; an estimate is not a delivery promise.

03 / A useful record

What belongs in a spreadsheet row?

Whether you save one item or compare several, a concise record should let you reopen the exact option and understand why you kept it.

Field What to record What it helps resolve
Source & identity Current seller URL, marketplace, item ID and date checked. A moved or dead link; a similar title leading to a different item.
Exact variant Colour name, size, style option and quantity. A listing containing several products under one headline.

04 / Direct answers

A few questions worth asking.

Short answers for the moment between discovering an item and deciding what to do next.

What is a PikoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a discovery list that groups product links and comparison notes for people researching purchases through PikoBuy. A useful entry identifies the source, variant and measurements. The list itself does not confirm stock, authenticity or delivery terms.

How do I use a PikoBuy spreadsheet?

Choose a category, shortlist a few relevant items, open the current source listing and confirm the exact option, measurements and total cost. Ask the service handling your order to confirm link support and current conditions before paying.

Are PikoBuy QC photos proof of authenticity?

No. QC photos can help you inspect visible details and compare an item with the selected listing. They do not establish brand authorization, provenance, material composition or long-term durability.

How should I compare shoe sizes across EU, UK and US charts?

Measure your foot in centimetres and use the chart for the exact model and seller. Check whether each measurement refers to foot length or insole length, and whether the US scale is men's or women's. Generic conversions are only a starting point.

Does an item price include international shipping?

Do not assume it does. Check the item price, domestic delivery, service charges, international freight and any destination charges separately. Confirm what the current quote includes and whether packed weight or dimensions can change it.

What should I do if a saved product link stops working?

Use your saved item ID, seller name and variant notes to locate the current listing. Compare its images and options again. A replacement URL or similar title does not guarantee that it is the same product.

Is this an official PikoBuy website?

No. This is an independent CAFANS editorial guide with no claimed affiliation to PikoBuy or the brands pictured. Navigation opens CAFANS or the specified CAFANS categories; it does not open a PikoBuy checkout.
